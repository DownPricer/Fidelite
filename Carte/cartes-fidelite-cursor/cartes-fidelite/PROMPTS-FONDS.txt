Génération : outil ImageGen intégré, en édition d'image (pas de CLI/API).
Les quatre images jointes ont servi de références. Les sorties sont fournies dans assets/.
Le code CSS effectue le cadrage d'affichage sans modifier les PNG générés.

[bronze]
Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset type: blank bronze loyalty card background for an HTML/CSS component. Input image 1 is the edit target. Remove ALL text (including MEMBRE, tier title, person's name, points, status sentence), the entire QR code, AND the entire progress bar and its recessed track. Seamlessly reconstruct the original brushed metal under these removed items. Preserve the brushed metal texture, color, lighting and shiny rounded border, and preserve the exact circular embossed emblem at upper right. Preserve the card's outer silhouette, its position and scale inside the image, the original canvas aspect ratio and existing transparent background outside the card; do not add any new external background or shadow. Front-on flat card. The entire area except the original top-right emblem and perimeter must now be uninterrupted blank brushed metal, with no letters, numbers, QR remnants, frames or progress track. Keep all surviving design details as close as possible to the original. Return ONE edited card image, not a montage.
